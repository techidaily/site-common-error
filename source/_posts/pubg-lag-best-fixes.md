## Fix 1: Change your download region

 In most cases, the Steam download problem might be related to the download server: the server is either faced with a technical issue or is congested with people downloading or updating. You can try to change your download region to fix this issue, by following these steps:

1. On your Steam client, click**Steam** , then click**Settings** .  
![](https://images.drivereasy.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/08/steam-settings.jpg)
2. Click **Downloads** , then click the drop-down menu of **Download Region** to choose a different region.  
![](https://images.drivereasy.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/08/steam-download-region-1.jpg)
3. Click **OK** to save the setting.

 Here the key is to find a location where there isn’t a lot of traffic. So you might have to keep switching download servers until you find one that works. But if it is not helping at all, simply move on to the next fix.

## Fix 3: Repair library folder

 The Steam library folder stores the files Steam needs in order to run your games. You may experience issues if you have outdated files or subfolders. To fix this problem, you can try to repair the library folder. Here’s how to do it:

1. Open the **Steam**  menu and select **Settings** .  
![](https://images.drivereasy.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/08/steam-settings.jpg)
2. Click**Downloads** . Then, select **STEAM LIBRARY FOLDERS** .  
![](https://images.drivereasy.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/08/steam-library-folder.jpg)
3. Click the**3 dots** on the right side, and select**Repair Folder** .  
![](https://images.drivereasy.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/08/3-dots-repair-folder.jpg)
4. After it’s done, click**Close** .  
![](https://images.drivereasy.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/08/repair-folder-done.jpg)

 Then try to download your game to test for the issue. If it remains, move on to the second method.

## Fix 4: Reset Windows proxy settings

 If none of the fixes above works for you, you can try to disable Windows proxy settings. Here’s how to do it:

1. Click the **Start** button and type**proxy** in the Windows search bar. Then select**Proxy settings** .  
![](https://images.drivereasy.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/08/proxy2.jpg)
2. Turn off the **Automatically detect settings** button.  
![](https://images.drivereasy.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/08/proxy-turn-off.jpg)

 After turning it off, go to check if your Steam works properly. If it doesn’t do the trick, simply move on to the next fix.

## Fix 6: Flush DNS cache

 An obsolete DNS resolver cache in your computer could also prevent your Steam from connecting to download servers, and deleting it may help. Here’s how to do it:

1. Click the **Start** button, and type **cmd** in the Windows search bar. Then right-click**Command Prompt** and select **Run as administrator** .  
![](https://images.drivereasy.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/08/cmd-command-prompt-modified-1.jpg)
2. When a pop-up box comes up, type**ipconfig /flushdns** . Then hit**enter** key. You should see a message which confirms that the DNS Resolver Cache has been flushed successfully.  
![](https://images.drivereasy.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/08/cmd-type.jpg)

Check if the issue persists. If not, check out the fix below.

## Fix 7: Verify the integrity of game files

 Sometimes, bad hardware, software crashes and power surges can cause corruption in the local files of the game, and consequently cause Steam downloads to stick at 0 bytes. Steam can verify the correctness over installed game files and fix them if necessary. Follow the instructions given below:

1. Navigate to the **Library** section of your Steam client,**right-click on the game** you are experiencing the issue with.
2. Select **Properties** .  
![](https://images.drivereasy.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/08/game-property.jpg)
3. Click**LOCAL FILES** and select the **Verify integrity of game files** option.  
![](https://images.drivereasy.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/08/local-files-verify-integrity.jpg)  
 But please note that this fix only works when the game has been downloaded a little bit before it gets stuck at 0 bytes, otherwise LOCAL FILES would not exist yet.

If the problem persists, there is one more fix you can try.

## Fix 8: Temporarily disable your antivirus software

 Your firewall or antivirus software can sometimes cause interference to the network connection of your Steam client.

 To see if that happens, temporarily disable your antivirus program for the duration of the Steam download and see if the issue persists. (You can consult your antivirus program documentation for instructions on disabling it.)

 If this resolves your problem, you can add Steam to the whitelist of your firewall by visiting its configuration pane. You can also contact the vendor of your firewall and ask for help.

 When your antivirus service is disabled, be careful about what sites you visit, what emails you open and what files you download.
