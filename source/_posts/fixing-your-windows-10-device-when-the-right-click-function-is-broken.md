## Windows 10 Light Dimmer Malfunction? Here's the Solution

![](https://images.drivereasy.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/07/img_595b0a627401c.png)Feel the brightness not proper on your Windows 10 screen? No change when you adjust it via brightness control? No annoyance anymore! Here’s the guide to tell you how to fix the**brightness not working issue on Windows 10** . Read on and find out how. In general, your brightness control not working issue is due to the display driver. Here are two solutions you can try:

1. **[Update your display driver](#s1)**
2. [**Use the Microsoft Basic Display Adapter software**](#s2)

## Solution 1: Update your display driver

There are two ways you can update your display driver : manually or automatically. **Manual driver update –** You can update your display driver manually by going to the manufacturer’s website for your display card  , and searching for the most recent correct driver. Be sure to choose only drivers that are compatible with your Windows version. **Automatic driver update –** If you don’t have the time, patience, or computer skills to update your display driver manually, you can, instead, do it automatically with **[Driver Easy](https://tools.techidaily.com/drivereasy/download/)**  . Adjust the brightness on your Windows 10 computer to see if it works now. If it works, so great! If it doesn’t, don’t worry, move on to Solution 2.

## Solution 2: Use Microsoft Basic Display Adapter software

1. Right-click on the Start button to select Device Manager.
2. Double-click **Display adapters** . Then right-click on your display adapter driver to select **Update driver** .![](https://images.drivereasy.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/07/img_595b0d3241de4.png)
3. Click **Browse my computer for driver software** .![](https://images.drivereasy.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/07/img_595b183012265.png)
4. Go on to choose**Let me pick from a list of available drivers on my computer** .![](https://images.drivereasy.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/07/img_595b1a726a5d9.png)
5. Click**Microsoft Basic Display Adapter** . Then click**Next** to continue.![](https://images.drivereasy.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/07/img_595b306d34eb6.png)
Now your Windows 10 computer will install the Microsoft Basic Display Adapter driver. When it’s done, reboot your computer and try again to adjust the brightness, the issue should be fixed now. Hopefully, this article helps you fix the problem. Feel free to comment below with your own experiences.
