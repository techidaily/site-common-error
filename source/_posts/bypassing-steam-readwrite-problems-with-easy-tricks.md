### Step 1\. Restart your network

1)

Shut down your computer.
  
2)

Unplug your modem and Wi-Fi router if you have one.
  
3)

Wait for about 30 seconds and plug in your modem and router back.
  
4)

Power on your computer and Log in Skype to try video call again.

If the error still exists, go on Step 2, please.
