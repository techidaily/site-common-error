## Solution 2: Use Microsoft Basic Display Adapter software

1. Right-click on the Start button to select Device Manager.
2. Double-click **Display adapters** . Then right-click on your display adapter driver to select **Update driver** .![](https://images.drivereasy.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/07/img_595b0d3241de4.png)
3. Click **Browse my computer for driver software** .![](https://images.drivereasy.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/07/img_595b183012265.png)
